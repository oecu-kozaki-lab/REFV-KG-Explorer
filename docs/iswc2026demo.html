REFV-KG Explorer

Causal and Stakeholder-Oriented Exploration of Renewable Energy Policy Discussions

Kouji Kozaki and Mizuki Hayashi

Osaka Electro-Communication University, Japan

REFV-KG Explorer connects causal statements about renewable energy policy with stakeholder opinions and their supporting passages. Explore causes, effects and countermeasures, compare who said what, and inspect the evidence in a coordinated table and graph view.

About the knowledge graph

REFV-KG contains 22,922 causal triples from 523 Japanese renewable energy policy documents. Of these triples, 79.6% have at least one related stakeholder opinion. The Explorer provides adjustable keyword granularity for an overview across documents or more detailed inspection.

The intermediate classes remain provisional. A task-based user study is future work. The system supports exploration and comparison of documented perspectives.
